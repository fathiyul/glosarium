**Founder burnout** is the physical and emotional depletion from sustained startup pressure: endless decisions, financial risk, identity fused with the company, loneliness at the top of a tiny org chart. It arrives as cynicism, dread, broken sleep and bad decisions — and it kills companies through their founders before the market gets a chance.

```text
warning signs:  dreading Mondays you once loved · snapping at co-founders · deciding nothing for weeks
                · fantasizing about quitting daily (vs occasionally, which is normal)
```

Prevention is structural, not motivational: sleep protected like runway, exercise scheduled like board meetings, co-founder or peer contact weekly (isolation accelerates everything bad), and a life outside the company that survives its death. Recovery, once deep, usually requires real rest — shortened weeks fail where sabbaticals succeed.

The classic mistakes:

- Treating rest as reward. "I'll rest after the launch/round/milestone" — there is always a next one. Rest is maintenance, scheduled regardless of milestones.
- Confusing burnout with failure feelings. Normal founder life includes fear and doubt; burnout is the inability to recover between sprints. Learn your own baseline so you notice the shift.
- Solo suffering. Hiding struggle from co-founders, investors and family multiplies it. Say it early to people who can help — most have been there and have practical fixes.
- Quitting the company to fix burnout (or vice versa). Sometimes the company is the problem (wrong business, wrong team) and rest will not fix it; sometimes you need rest, not resignation. Distinguish with outside perspective — therapist, coach, brutally honest peer.
- Glorifying the grind publicly. Hustle theater sets expectations (team, investors, self) that make sane pacing feel like slacking. Model sustainable intensity instead.

**Structural fixes beat willpower:** [maker time protected](/t/maker-vs-manager-schedule), decisions delegated, personal [runway](/t/personal-runway) honest, and permission — from yourself — to rest without guilt. The company needs you functional more than it needs you present.
