**PSE (Penyelenggara Sistem Elektronik)** registration lists a company as an electronic system operator with the Indonesian government (through the communications ministry). Platforms, apps, SaaS products and significant websites operating in Indonesia are expected to register — it is the state's inventory of who runs digital systems touching Indonesian users and data.

```text
operate digital service in Indonesia → register as PSE → obligations attach (data, content, access)
```

Registration is the beginning, not the end: it connects to content-moderation duties, data-protection obligations ([UU PDP](/t/uu-pdp)), and potential access-blocking exposure for non-compliance. Foreign platforms serving Indonesia face their own registration expectations — "servers abroad" does not exempt the obligation.

The classic mistakes:

- Ignoring it as paperwork. Unregistered operation risks access blocking — the site simply becomes unreachable in Indonesia. For a consumer product, that is death by bureaucracy; register early.
- Registering and stopping. The duties attached (content handling, data rules, reporting) need ongoing processes, not a one-time filing. Assign ownership.
- Assuming foreign incorporation exempts. Serving Indonesian users triggers expectations regardless of where the company sits. Map obligations by user location, not company location.
- DIY on scope questions. Whether a given product, API or internal tool counts, and which category applies, repays an hour of expert advice against months of wrong assumptions.

**Register early, maintain continuously**, and pair it with real [privacy practice](/t/privacy-policy) — the filing proves registration, only operations prove compliance.
