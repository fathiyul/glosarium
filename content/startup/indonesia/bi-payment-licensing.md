BI payment licensing (PJP) is the regime under which Bank Indonesia — Indonesia's central bank, which regulates payment *systems* — authorizes companies to provide payment services. Anything that moves money for others sits inside it: e-wallets, payment initiation and execution, acquiring, remittance, the rails a [payment gateway](/t/payment-gateway) runs on. "PJP" (Penyedia Jasa Pembayaran) is the license category itself.

The shape of the regime (verify current rules — details change, and this is not legal advice): Bank Indonesia assesses licensees on a risk basis, with approval requirements scaling to the risk profile of the services you run — capital adequacy, governance, consumer protection, AML/CFT controls, data handling, and increasingly operational resilience and interoperability obligations (the national QR standard [QRIS](/t/qris) is part of the ecosystem). Foreign participation is possible within the framework's limits; many founders route through partnering with a licensed bank or existing license holder precisely because standing up the full licensing path takes time and capital.

What founders actually get wrong:

- **Building the money-moving product first, asking license-shaped questions later.** If your roadmap includes holding balances, executing payouts or acquiring merchants, the licensing conversation belongs in the first board cycle, not the pre-launch panic ([regulatory risk](/t/regulatory-risk) in costume).
- **Underestimating the entity and paperwork layer** — a local [PT](/t/pt-perseroan-terbatas) (or PMA structure for foreign-owned lines, with its own minimum investment thresholds), NIB/business registration via [OSS](/t/oss-rba), and the sector codes that match your activity ([KBLI](/t/kbli)).
- **Assuming BI and OJK are interchangeable.** Payment systems sit with BI; financial *services* (lending, insurance, securities) with [OJK](/t/ojk-licensing). A fintech often needs to know which activity touches which regulator.

The honest strategy: map each product feature to the regulated activity it performs; decide build-license vs. ride-an-existing-license (bank partnerships, sponsor models); and treat the regulator relationship like an enterprise account — documented, proactive, patient. Companies that clear PJP requirements early gain exactly the boring advantage: they can still be doing business the quarter the sweep notices everyone else. Confirm scope, thresholds and current requirements with Indonesian counsel and BI's published regulations — the regime updates.
