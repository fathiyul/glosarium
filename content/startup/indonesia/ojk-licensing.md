OJK licensing is the authorization Indonesia's Financial Services Authority (Otoritas Jasa Keuangan) requires to run regulated financial businesses: banking, insurance, multifinance/pension funds, securities markets, and the fintech categories it supervises — most famously [P2P lending](/t/p2p-lending-license), and now also crypto-asset trading, whose oversight moved from the commodity regulator to OJK in recent years. If BI covers payment *systems*, OJK covers financial *services*: products that take credit risk, invest client money, or sell risk itself.

Practical shape (confirm current rules with counsel — this changes): registration-then-license paths, minimum capital that steps up over time, fit-and-proper tests for owners and management, consumer protection rules (marketing ethics, collection conduct, transparency of all-in cost), reporting and IT governance. Lending platforms have faced explicit caps and conduct rules around interest, debt collection and data use — the era of unregistered "fintech lending" apps shutting down mid-loan was a warning to anyone building consumer credit on goodwill.

The founder decision tree:

```text
moving money only .................... BI (PJP) — see bi-payment-licensing
lending / investing / insuring money . OJK licensing/registration
both (wallet + credit) ............... both regulators, and a
                                       company that answers to each
```

The classic mistakes: copying a lending product from a deck without modeling license timelines (approval can take longer than your seed runway — plan sponsor arrangements with licensed entities in the meantime); letting "we're a technology platform" framing collide with what regulators call intermediating money or risk; and treating consumer-protection rules as a compliance slide rather than a product constraint ([data](/t/uu-pdp) handling, collections, disclosure all become UX decisions).

OJK-supervised status, once earned, is a genuine moat in a crowded market — many competitors cannot legally operate. Budget for it as a multi-quarter project with local legal and compliance advisors, not a form.
