The **NPWP (Nomor Pokok Wajib Pajak)** is the taxpayer identification number every Indonesian company (and tax-resident individual) needs: it identifies the company to the tax office for filings, payments, withholding ([PPh](/t/pph-withholding)) and VAT ([PKP/PPN](/t/pkp-ppn)) matters. Banks and counterparties ask for it alongside the [NIB](/t/nib); without one the company cannot properly withhold, invoice with VAT, or file.

```text
company NPWP:  for corporate filings, withholding obligations, VAT registration
founder/director NPWP: personal filings; foreign staff have their own considerations
```

Get the company's NPWP immediately after formation — backdated tax obligations do not wait for paperwork, and clean tax standing is checked in every fundraise and partnership [due diligence](/t/investor-due-diligence).

The classic mistakes:

- Operating on personal NPWP. Company revenue through personal tax identity mixes liabilities and blocks proper bookkeeping. Separate from day one.
- No withholding discipline. Paying vendors and salaries gross without withholding the required PPh creates arrears plus penalties discovered years later. Withhold correctly from the first payment (see [bookkeeping](/t/bookkeeping)).
- Missing the VAT threshold decision. Crossing into mandatory [PKP](/t/pkp-ppn) without registering (or registering pointlessly early) both cost money. Track revenue against the threshold as you grow.
- Founder tax blindness. Salary vs dividends vs capital gains are taxed differently, and founder exits have real tax bills. Personal tax planning belongs beside company planning, with an accountant — see [startup taxes](/t/startup-taxes).

**Get an accountant early** — monthly bookkeeping with correct withholding beats heroic annual cleanup every time.
