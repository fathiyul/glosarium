An **ICP license (filing)** is required to host websites on mainland Chinese servers: without it, sites face blocking or must serve China from abroad (slowly, unreliably). Commercial operations need the full license; the filing layer covers informational presence. Either way, the Chinese internet treats unlicensed hosting as simply not fully there.

```text
serve China properly → mainland hosting → ICP filing/license → compliant operation
alternatives:  Hong Kong/Singapore hosting (latency + blocking risk) · local partnerships
```

Foreign entities face additional friction: licensing typically runs through domestic entities, which is one reason market-entry structures (including [VIE arrangements](/t/china-vie-structure) where applicable) dominate planning conversations. The license is thus rarely a standalone task — it arrives bundled with entity, hosting and compliance decisions.

The classic mistakes:

- Launching unlicensed and hoping. Blocking arrives without warning or appeal timeline, usually right after traction makes it painful. License before marketing spend, not after.
- Assuming global cloud regions suffice. Nearby regions serve China poorly (latency, packet loss, intermittent blocking) for anything interactive. Mainland presence is an infrastructure decision with licensing attached.
- Content rules blindness. Licensed hosting brings content obligations (prohibited categories, takedown responsiveness, real-name systems where applicable). Operating blind invites suspension regardless of paperwork status.
- DIY entity-plus-license tangles. Foreign ownership, hosting, licensing and data rules interact densely. Local counsel plus local hosting partner from the planning stage — this stack rewards guides and punishes improvisers.
- One China strategy forever. Rules, platforms and enforcement moods shift materially across years. Revisit posture annually with local advice rather than running on entry-year assumptions.

**Sequence:** entity decision → hosting architecture → ICP process → content compliance posture → launch spend. Skipping straight to spend builds on sand the state can wash away overnight.
