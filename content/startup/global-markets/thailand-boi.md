Thailand's **BOI (Board of Investment)** grants qualifying projects tax holidays, import-duty relief, land-ownership and work-permit facilitation — meaningful economics for tech companies manufacturing, running data operations or building regional tech presence. Incentives scale with activity type, location and investment size; software/services access is narrower than promoted headlines suggest.

```text
fit check:  qualifying activity + investment threshold + location → apply pre-commitment
value:      tax holidays + duty relief + permits facilitation (terms per certificate)
```

Apply before committing spend — incentives reward planned investment, rarely retroactive splurges. And model the compliance tail (reporting, conditions, audits) against the benefit honestly; incentives with heavy tails suit large commitments, not exploratory outposts.

The classic mistakes:

- Structuring around hoped incentives. Founding the Thai entity shape on assumed approval, then operating noncompliant while waiting. Approvals first, structure second, always.
- Ignoring zone/location tiers. Benefit depth varies by location — industrial-zone reality differs from Bangkok-headquarters convenience. Optimize jointly with hiring and logistics, not incentives alone.
- Underestimating documentation. Applications, progress reports and compliance evidence consume real founder and finance time. Price administration against benefit like any grant ([non-dilutive discipline](/t/non-dilutive-funding)).
- One-incentive dependence. Policy, zones and qualifying lists shift with governments. Diversify rationale for Thai presence (market, talent, costs) beyond any single scheme.
- Transfer-pricing blindness. Cross-border flows inside incentivized structures attract scrutiny by design. Document arm's-length logic from the first intercompany ringgit ([transfer pricing](/t/transfer-pricing)).

**Use BOI as:** an accelerant for genuinely-planned Thai presence — applied early, modeled honestly, complied with boringly. Compare [Malaysia](/t/malaysia-digital-status) and [Singapore schemes](/t/singapore-startup-grants) when placing regional bets.
