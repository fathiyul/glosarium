---
title: "Regional Pricing"
summary: "Different prices per country to match local purchasing power and competition."
levels: { tech-entrepreneur: growth }
---

Regional pricing means charging different rates in different countries — typically lower list prices in markets with lower purchasing power, so the product is actually buyable there. A $50/month tool that clears minimum wage expectations in Singapore may be a month's income for an Indonesian user; the alternative to a local price is not full price, it is piracy, no payment method, or a local competitor at $8.

The economics are real: revenue per account drops, but so does [CAC](/t/unit-economics) in crowded local markets and the served segment grows. Keep the *support and product cost* honest though — a lower price tier that still consumes full support volume is a margin trap ([contribution margin](/t/contribution-margin) per country, not blended, is the number that decides).

The failure modes:

- **Arbitrage without a plan.** VPN purchases, a foreign company buying at the local rate, resellers in cheap regions undercutting rich ones. Decide your tolerance and enforce with billing country + tax/VAT signals ([cross-border digital tax](/t/cross-border-digital-tax)), not IP alone; perfect enforcement is impossible and over-engineering it costs more than leakage.
- **Price reveals the discount.** Publishing "70% off Indonesia" invites global buyers to ask "why not me?" — many companies localize quietly via currency and plan naming rather than a discount percentage.
- **Payment reality ignored.** Local pricing means local payment methods too — cards alone will cap you at the affluent minority; e-wallets, bank transfer and local rails matter ([payment gateway](/t/payment-gateway), [Indonesia payment methods](/t/indonesia-payment-methods)).

When not to: B2B sold centrally (procurement pays in dollars anyway; global contracts set one price), or where your competitive position is premium-everywhere ([counter-positioning](/t/counter-positioning) relies on that consistency). The honest middle for most [SEA expansion](/t/regional-expansion-sea): segment- and country-specific tiers, reviewed for margin quarterly, with local competitor pricing and willingness-to-pay tests as inputs, not vibes.
