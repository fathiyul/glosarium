Malaysia Digital (MD) status is a government incentive designation for tech and digital companies, administered by MDEC — the agency that inherited and replaced the older MSC Malaysia status. Companies that qualify gain a package of benefits aimed at making Malaysia a competitive base for digital business, instead of the default Singapore.

What the status typically offers (exact incentives and criteria change with each policy round — check MDEC's current published terms, and this is not legal advice):

```text
tax:          incentive rates on qualifying income for
              approved activities (reduced or exemption tiers)
ownership:    facilitation for 100% foreign equity in eligible
              activities
talent:       expedited visas/passes for the company's foreign
              knowledge workers — the Malaysia counterpart to
              the foreign-permit friction elsewhere in SEA
reporting:    commitments in return — local knowledge-worker
              hiring, activity reporting, sometimes investment
              or revenue targets
```

Who should care: founders choosing an [SEA holding structure](/t/singapore-holding-structure) who want a cheaper, less-crowded base than Singapore while keeping English-language business, decent infrastructure and Muslim-market proximity (see [halal certification](/t/halal-certification) dynamics if your product touches food/consumer); companies with Malaysian revenue who want the talent-pass facilitation for regional staff.

The honest trade-offs: the application and renewal cycle is real work — MD comes with reporting obligations and activity conditions, so budget compliance like a feature; incentives bind to *approved activities*, so a pivot can change the tax story; and status does not replace the [Sdn Bhd](/t/malaysia-sdn-bhd) incorporation, SST/VAT obligations ([malaysia-sst](/t/malaysia-sst)) or [PDPA](/t/pdpa-malaysia) data duties — it layers on top of them.

Treat MD as one input in the [country-differences](/t/sea-country-differences) calculation: where the incentives materially beat running the same team from Singapore or KL without them, apply early (the paperwork clock starts before the benefit does); where they are a rounding error, do not let a status chase delay product work.
