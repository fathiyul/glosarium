International expansion is entering new country markets — the move that turns a successful single-country company into a regional one, and the move that burns more premature startups than most failure causes. Done deliberately it follows a logic: expansion is for when the *home engine* is proven and the *new market* fits the model; neither condition is optional.

The entry decision in practice:

```text
sequence:   similar markets before large ones — language, legal,
            payment and buying-behavior distance compound
            (SEA founders often run ID → MY/SG/TH/VN by profile,
            see sea-country-differences)
vehicle:    representative office → local entity vs EOR/PPO for
            hiring → full subsidiary with local substance
localization: payments (e-wallets, bank transfer, COD behavior),
            pricing currency + tax, content/support hours,
            compliance by country (data, licensing, employment)
team:       one credible local owner beats ten remote assignments
```

The classic mistakes, ranked by how often they kill:

- **Transplanting the home playbook.** What converted in Jakarta does not convert in Manila unchanged — payments ([indonesia-payment-methods](/t/indonesia-payment-methods) type realities), sales motion, trust sources differ. Expansion without market learning is expensive tourism.
- **Premature country revenue.** A new market with flat early revenue and real fixed costs (entity, legal, hires) drags [burn multiple](/t/burn-multiple) and focus; if the home market is not yet proven, expansion multiplies an unproven thing.
- **Compliance as afterthought.** Entity requirements, foreign-worker rules ([foreign-worker-permit](/t/foreign-worker-permit)), data laws ([uu-pdp](/t/uu-pdp), [pdpa-malaysia](/t/pdpa-malaysia)), tax registration ([cross-border digital tax](/t/cross-border-digital-tax)) and licensing ([thailand-boi](/t/thailand-boi) incentives and gates exist) are the expansion budget, not the travel.

The structural note: holding and entity architecture ([singapore-holding-structure](/t/singapore-holding-structure), [delaware-flip](/t/delaware-flip) for US-facing, per-market entities) should be chosen with counsel before revenue or hires cross borders, because fixing it later is a legal project.

When expansion *is* right: home market saturated or ceiling known, the product's value is market-portable (language/local fit aside), and you can staff a real local owner. When not: as a story for investors, or as a distraction from a retention problem at home — the two reasons it most often gets started.
