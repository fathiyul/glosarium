Cross-border digital tax is the set of rules that make you collect and remit consumption tax (VAT/GST) — and sometimes income tax — when you sell digital services to customers in countries where you have no office. The world largely settled on the principle that digital services are taxed *where the customer is*, and regulators gave foreign sellers a way to comply without a local entity: simplified registration regimes.

For a SaaS founder billing internationally, the practical map:

- **Consumption tax.** Many jurisdictions now require foreign digital sellers above a revenue threshold to register, charge local VAT/GST, and file — thresholds and rates vary and change often. [Indonesia taxes digital services of foreign providers](/t/pkp-ppn) through appointed collectors; similar regimes exist across the region ([Malaysia SST](/t/malaysia-sst)). Registration often does not create a local entity — it is a tax account, not incorporation.
- **Withholding.** B2B payments in several markets carry withholding on services and royalties — your invoice may arrive net of a percentage ([Indonesia PPh withholding](/t/pph-withholding)); grossing-up clauses and documentation (tax residence certificates, treaty relief) decide whether the customer or you eat that.
- **Permanent establishment.** Beyond tax-collection territory: a local team selling and concluding contracts can create a taxable presence. Keep the structure question to your advisor before hiring in-country.

The classic mistakes: assuming "we have no office there" means no obligation (the threshold usually triggers on revenue *to* the country, not presence *in* it); ignoring withholding until cash mysteriously arrives short; and letting pricing ignore tax — a 10% local VAT on your $50 list may make the product $55 to the buyer, so [regional pricing](/t/regional-pricing) must be after-tax honest.

None of this is legal advice and every country cited changes rules frequently — the durable move is a tax advisor per major market before revenue there gets meaningful, and pricing set knowing what actually lands.
