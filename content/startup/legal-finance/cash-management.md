title: "Cash Management"
summary: "Where idle company money sits, and why it should not sit still."

---

Cash management is running the company's money between payroll dates deliberately: where cash sits, how liquid it is, whose signatures move it, and how currency and duration match obligations. Idle cash is wasted runway; mismanaged cash turns a funding problem into a fraud or a locked-balance problem.

Three practices separate a managed treasury from a checking account with ambition:

- **Separate the balances.** Keep the operating buffer (a couple of months of [burn](/t/burn-rate)) liquid in a plain account; put the rest of the [runway](/t/runway) in higher-yield but still liquid instruments — government bills or term deposits laddered so a slice matures regularly. Chasing yield with runway money is not investing; it is adding risk to the money you must spend.
- **Match currency to obligation.** Salaries are paid in rupiah; a dollar balance carrying a currency move can shorten runway without spending a cent extra. For multi-country teams, this gets worse ([cross-border digital tax](/t/cross-border-digital-tax), transfer timing).
- **Control the movement.** Payment fraud targets exactly this function — impersonated vendors, forged invoices, "urgent CEO" transfers. Dual sign-off above thresholds, callback verification for changed bank details, and separation of who initiates and who approves are cheap and stop most losses.

The classic mistakes: letting the runway pile up unmanaged while [bookkeeping](/t/bookkeeping) shows it as "cash," then discovering the yield chase froze it right before payroll; or the opposite — treating every dollar as needing to stay in a zero-interest account because finance felt scary, losing real value to inflation quietly.

When not to over-engineer: pre-revenue with one bank account and two founders, the controls still matter (shared credentials, no surprises), but ladders and sweep programs can wait until there is enough idle cash for yield to cover the effort. The moment you raise meaningful money, "where exactly is it and who can move it" becomes board-level; have the answer ready.
