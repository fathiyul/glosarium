**Incorporation** creates the legal person that owns the product, signs contracts, hires people and takes investment. The choices made here — entity type and country — echo for the company's whole life: they set taxes, fundraising options, hiring rules and how painful a future restructuring ([flip](/t/singapore-flip)) will be.

```text
decide:  where customers and investors are → pick entity investors fund → form early, cheaply
avoid:   exotic structures for a pre-revenue team; incorporating nowhere ("we'll do it later")
```

Default to the boring standard your likely investors fund: a [Delaware C-Corp](/t/delaware-c-corp) for US venture, a [Singapore Pte Ltd](/t/singapore-pte-ltd) for regional rounds, a [PT](/t/pt-perseroan-terbatas) operating in Indonesia. Clever structures optimise for futures that may never arrive, at the cost of complexity now.

The classic mistakes:

- Incorporating nowhere. Founders build for a year as individuals, then discover the IP belongs to no company, contracts are personal, and investors need months of cleanup. Form before traction, not after.
- Fancy structure too early. Offshore holdings with no revenue and no investors cost money yearly and confuse everyone. Start simple where you operate; restructure when a round requires it.
- Founders' IP outside the company. Code written before formation belongs to whoever wrote it until assigned. Every founder and contractor signs [IP assignment](/t/ip-assignment) at formation.
- DIY across borders. One country's routine filing is another's trap (founder visas, foreign ownership limits, tax residency). Local counsel at formation is cheap; cross-border cleanup is not.

**Talk to a notary, lawyer or tax consultant for your case** before choosing — entity, country and timing interact, and this page cannot see your situation.

## In Indonesia and Southeast Asia

Indonesian founders typically form a [PT](/t/pt-perseroan-terbatas) (or [PT Perorangan](/t/pt-perorangan) solo), get [NIB](/t/nib) licensing and [NPWP](/t/npwp) tax numbers early. Teams raising regionally often add a [Singapore holding structure](/t/singapore-holding-structure) before a priced round — do it when investors ask, not speculatively.
