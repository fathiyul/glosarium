Customer success is the organized function of making customers get value so they renew and expand — proactive, owned, measured — as opposed to support, which reacts to problems. It exists because SaaS revenue is a subscription: the deal is signed *against a promise*, and the promise is tested every month whether anyone watches or not.

The core loop is simple: define what value looks like per segment (an adoption milestone within the first weeks), instrument health against it (usage, goals hit, support load, exec relationships), intervene when health dips, and drive renewals and expansion from the pattern, not from surprise calls. The classic failure that creates churn is a customer going quiet for two quarters — no tickets (they gave up), no usage, no one notices until the cancellation lands.

The classic mistakes:

- **CS as reactive support.** A team that answers tickets and reminds people to pay owns nothing about outcomes. Health scores that nobody acts on are dashboards, not programs.
- **Uniform coverage.** Enterprise logos get a named CSM; the long tail gets [product-led](/t/product-led-growth) nudges, guides and in-app check-ins. Scaling means matching touch to contribution, and pricing the high-touch accordingly.
- **Expansion as a campaign.** Upsell worked into a renewal-scramble feels like a tax. Expansion is a natural output of value ([expansion revenue](/t/expansion-revenue) is what working CS shows up as in [NRR](/t/net-revenue-retention)).

The tradeoff is real money: CSMs are expensive; at SMB prices they destroy [contribution margin](/t/contribution-margin) while saving churn that programmatic onboarding might have prevented anyway. The other tradeoff is organizational: CS between product and sales hears every complaint and controls none of the fixes — give it a path to escalate (health data feeds the roadmap) or it becomes a complaint inbox with a nicer title.

When not to build it: pre-PMF, founders own this directly (it is the best learning you can buy); and in one-shot purchase businesses, there is no renewal to protect.
