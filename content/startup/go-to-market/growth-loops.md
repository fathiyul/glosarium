---
title: "Growth Loops"
summary: "Self-reinforcing cycles where outputs of growth feed new growth, as opposed to linear funnels."
levels: { tech-entrepreneur: growth }
---

A growth loop is a cycle where the output of growth feeds its input: users create content that attracts users; referrals bring users who refer; spend buys data that buys cheaper spend. Against the classic funnel — where growth leaks and every period restarts from zero — a closed loop compounds: same inputs, bigger outputs, period after period.

```text
funnel:  spend → acquire → (leaks) → next period: spend again
loop:    acquire → activate → output (referral / content / data)
                     ↑_______________ feeds ______________↓
```

The honest framing is loop *rate*: what share of the output carries into the next cycle. A referral loop with k=0.3 (each user brings 0.3) does not self-replicate, but it reduces the gap paid acquisition must fill — every point is compounding on a base. [Viral loops](/t/viral-loop) are the headline case; content/SEO loops (articles → signups → case studies → articles), community loops ([community-led growth](/t/community-led-growth)), paid-data loops and two-sided-marketplace loops ([supply attracts demand attracts supply](/t/two-sided-marketplace)) are the everyday ones. Most healthy companies run two or three stacked loops plus spend.

The classic failures: calling a funnel a loop (blog → signups with no mechanism returning output to input is just content marketing); counting vanity loops (traffic compounding while conversion does not); and optimizing a loop whose input ceiling is tiny — a referral program on a product people won't mention has no fuel regardless of mechanics. The first check is always: does the loop's output reliably feed the top, measurably?

Loops fail upstream too often: the mechanism works but activation is broken, so the second cohort never produces output to feed the third. Fix [PMF](/t/product-market-fit) before loop engineering.

When not to bother: pre-retention stage (a leaky loop just burns money twice), and markets where your growth must come from a channel you cannot loop yet (enterprise, regulation-gated) — [word-of-mouth](/t/word-of-mouth) is the loop you earn there, eventually.
