**Impact** is the **change you cause that matters to users, the team or the business**. It's different from **output**, which is how much you produce (lines of code, tickets closed, hours worked). You can have lots of output and little impact, or modest output with great impact.

| Output (activity) | Impact (outcome) |
|---|---|
| "I closed 30 tickets" | "Checkout failures dropped from 2% to 0.3%" |
| "I wrote a new service" | "Customers get their reports in seconds instead of hours" |
| "I answered many questions" | "New hires reach their first PR in 3 days instead of 2 weeks" |
| "I refactored the module" | "Releases in that area no longer break, and changes there take half as long" |

Impact is about **results that others can see and care about**.

## How to increase it

- **Pick the right problems.** The biggest lever isn't working harder, but working on things that matter. Understand what the team and company are trying to achieve ([product thinking](/t/product-thinking)).
- **Ask "so what?"** Why does this task matter, and who benefits?
- **Solve root causes**, not symptoms ([root cause analysis](/t/root-cause-analysis)).
- **Finish and ship.** Half-done work has no impact. Follow it all the way to users and check the result ([ownership](/t/ownership)).
- **Multiply others.** Reviews, mentoring, docs, tooling and removing blockers raise a whole team's output. This "glue work" counts, though it's easy to overlook ([glue work](/t/glue-work)).
- **Reduce toil and risk**: automating a painful process or preventing an outage is impact.
- **Measure.** Define what success looks like before you start, and check afterwards.
- **Say no** to low-value work ([saying no](/t/saying-no), [prioritization](/t/prioritization)).

## Making it visible

Good work that nobody knows about doesn't help your career or the team's priorities. Record **what you did, why it mattered and the result**, with numbers where you can ([brag document](/t/brag-document)). Share outcomes in updates and reviews ([status updates](/t/status-updates)). This isn't bragging. It's helping others understand value, and it's what promotion and performance discussions look at ([performance review](/t/performance-review), [promotion](/t/promotion)).

## Impact grows with level

At junior level, impact is reliable delivery of well-scoped tasks. At mid-level, it's owning features end to end. At senior and beyond, it's shaping direction, improving how teams work and making others more effective ([scope of work](/t/scope-of-work)).

Don't confuse impact with visibility or heroics. Quiet prevention often beats dramatic rescue.
